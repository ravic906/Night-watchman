Patrol in the dark with only a flashlight, follow the radio's orders and keep the place standing until the day guard arrives at 6 AM.

• Four places to guard, each with its own twist

  – Apartment Block: nervous residents call the intercom – answer in time
  – Shopping Mall: close the roll-down shutters; thieves force them open
  – Factory: thieves start the machines to drown out their footsteps
• Lock the doors, arm the alarm, keep the power on and board up smashed windows – then watch the thieves undo it all
• Catch thieves in your beam to scare them off – the alarm tells you where they're running with the loot
• Something else walks the halls. It only moves when your light isn't on it.
• Hourly patrol orders, a heartbeat that quickens with your fear, and nights that get busier towards dawn
• Easy, Normal and Hard; best scores for every location
• Fully offline. No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected.
