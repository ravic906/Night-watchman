Night Watchman – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Night Watchman is an offline single-player game. This policy explains what the game does with your information: in short, nothing leaves your device.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it requests no Android permissions.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings (sound, location, difficulty) and your best scores in a small file inside the app's private storage on your device. This file never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Internet use

The game works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.
